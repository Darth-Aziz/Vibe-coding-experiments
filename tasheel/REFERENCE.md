# Reference archives (optional, local only)

The Next.js app in this folder is the **canonical** Tasheel implementation.

**`design/`** and **`service_LC/`** (if present on your machine) are **legacy Vite / Figma exports** kept for design archaeology. They are **not tracked in Git** and **excluded from ESLint and TypeScript** so `npm run lint` and `npm run build` stay focused on the real app.

To reproduce a clean clone: you do not need these directories.
